EmployeeHub HR Policy

1. Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted to the reporting manager for approval.
Employees should request planned leave at least 5 working days in advance.

2. Working Hours
The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Employees receive a one-hour lunch break.

3. Remote Work
Employees may request remote work with prior approval from their reporting manager.
Remote work arrangements depend on team requirements and company approval.

4. Employee Benefits
Employees can contact the HR department for information about applicable benefits.
Eligibility and benefit details depend on the employee's role and company policy.
